Five Product Highlights

The "no trusted central server" architecture thesis

The core claim isn't literally "zero servers anywhere," but "no single trusted server": matching, authentication, custody, and other core business logic never run on a szpL2E-operated, unilaterally controlled server — smart contracts and off-chain protocols replace the traditional backend API.

01

Five-platform, single-source client

Runs on iPhone, Android, Windows, macOS, and Wasm Web from one Rust core deployed everywhere, avoiding fragmented security logic.

02

Decentralized storage layer

IPFS plus Storacha (Filecoin persistence) as the content layer; content is encrypted and content-addressed on-chain, with conditional decryption after purchase — IP never passes through a szpL2E server gate.

03

szpEX Sonzpo trading wallet

Connects to hardware wallets; integrates CoW Protocol (best same-chain pricing and MEV protection) and Wormhole Bridge (cross-chain transfers), with a planned social-recovery mechanism.

04

szpDRX digital licensing exchange

Lets users publish digital IP such as e-books; ERC-1155/721 + EIP-2981 royalty standards guarantee automatic creator royalties even on secondary-market resale.

05

szpERX digital learning exchange

Course publishing and trading, anti-cheat online exams (behavioral fingerprinting, spaced-repetition testing, optional zk-SNARK privacy proofs), and milestone achievement NFTs.

06

Horizontal expansion roadmap

szpDRX and szpERX are two applications of the same asset-tization framework, enabling low-marginal-cost expansion into ERP, prediction markets, and gaming modules.

Core Positioning

What to learn, what to earn, who pays

The product runs a "multi-track parallel" content strategy across eleven learning tracks, each with its own verification mechanism and token reward curve.

What to learn

Eleven learning tracks — language, programming, finance, health literacy, crypto/Web3, ERP, digital content licensing, prediction markets/gaming, game design, AI co-work, and industry-specific skills — weighted 1x–10x by verification strength to deter grinding-for-tokens abuse.

What to earn

Four asset types: the platform token SZP, lottery entries, a portable learning résumé, and on-chain verifiable credentials (NFT/SBT).

Who pays

Four mutually reinforcing revenue sources: advertisers (precision placement in learning breaks), corporate sponsorship (recruiting and training tasks), school/government grants (education-policy goals), and paid user subscriptions (unlock advanced content).
